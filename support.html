Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a driveway to send its top cat out. If it matches the bus at the curb, it hops on. If not, it waits on the curb. Three cats fill a bus and it drives off. If the curb fills up and no cat can reach the bus, you are stuck, so plan ahead.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. The No Ads Pass and Starter Pack restore on any device. Coins and boosters are used up when bought and do not transfer between devices.

How do I turn off ads?

Ads in Tabby Traffic are optional reward videos you choose to watch. The No Ads Pass in the Shop skips them and gives the reward instantly.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account you used.

Can I reset my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
