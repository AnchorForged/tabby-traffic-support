Tabby Traffic Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Publisher: Tim Vaughan (AnchorForged) · Contact: chieflyappsupport@anchorforged.com

Tabby Traffic is a puzzle game. We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and you do not need an account to play.

What the game stores

Your progress (level, coins, stars, boosters, settings) is saved on your device only.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

Ads

The game shows optional reward videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. On iOS, AdMob uses your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when the game asks. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you buy the No Ads Pass, the game stops requesting ads. Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played: which levels players reach, where they get stuck, and which features they use. It records gameplay events and basic device information (model, iOS version, country from IP address) under a random app instance ID. We do not send your name, email or contacts. Firebase uses your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when the game asks. Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Tabby Traffic is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can delete all game data by deleting the app. To ask about data held by RevenueCat or AdMob in connection with this game, email us at the address above.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date.
